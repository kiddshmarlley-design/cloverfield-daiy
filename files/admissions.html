Admissions & Financial Aid

Applying to Ashcombe

Admission is need-blind for all domestic applicants, and every admitted student's demonstrated need is met in full. Below is exactly what happens between now and your decision letter.

1
Rolling — opens August 1

Submit the Common Application

Include the Ashcombe supplement: one 400-word essay on a question you've changed your mind about.

2
Within 3 weeks of submission

Transcripts & recommendations arrive

Two teacher recommendations and one counselor letter. We'll email you if anything is missing.

3
Optional, by invitation

Interview with an alum

Not required, but requested by about 40% of applicants each cycle — conducted by video or in person.

4
January 15 (Regular Decision)

Committee review

Three faculty and two admissions officers read every file in full — no algorithmic pre-screening.

5
Late March

Decisions released

Admitted students receive a full financial aid package the same day as their offer of admission.

Common questions

Before you write to us

Is Ashcombe test-optional?

Yes. SAT and ACT scores are considered only if submitted; roughly half of each admitted class does not submit a score.

What's the acceptance rate?

Around 19% for the most recent entering class, though we'd rather you focus on fit than on that number.

Do you offer merit scholarships?

No — all aid at Ashcombe is need-based, determined by the same office that reviews your admission file.

Can I transfer in from another college?

Yes, a limited number of transfer students are admitted each spring for the following fall term.
