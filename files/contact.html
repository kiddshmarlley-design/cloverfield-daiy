Visiting campus

Ashcombe is 212 River Road, Glenford, Vermont — about ninety minutes from Burlington and two hours from Montreal.

Campus tours run Monday through Saturday at 10:00 and 2:00 during the academic year, departing from the Founders Hall lobby. No appointment is required for prospective students; groups larger than six should email admissions in advance.

By car

From I-89, take Exit 12 and follow River Road two miles east. Visitor parking is behind Founders Hall.

By bus

The Glenford campus shuttle meets the Vermont Translines stop at Glenford Depot four times daily.
