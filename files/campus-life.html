Residential Life

Life along the Millbrook

Ashcombe is residential for all four years. Students live in one of six houses, eat in one dining hall, and — by design — run into their professors at the campus store more often than in office hours.

HOUSING

Six houses, four years

Students are sorted into one of six residential houses as first-years and stay there through graduation — each house elects its own council and hosts a termly formal.

DINING

One dining hall, three cooked meals

Ashcombe Commons serves all 1,650 students under one roof, with a standing rule that professors eat there too — no faculty dining room exists.
