CARD 01 · HISTORY

Founded on a dairy farm

Ashcombe opened in 1887 in a converted barn with 14 students and one professor of rhetoric. The barn is now the campus library.

CARD 02 · FACULTY

No teaching assistants

Every lecture, seminar, and lab section at Ashcombe is taught by a member of the faculty — never a graduate assistant.

CARD 03 · OUTCOMES

94% in field within a year

Ninety-four percent of Ashcombe graduates are employed or in graduate study within twelve months of commencement.

CARD 04 · CAMPUS

212 wooded acres

The campus sits along the Millbrook River, with teaching gardens, a working orchard, and six miles of trail used for coursework in ecology.
