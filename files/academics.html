Shared requirements

What every Ashcombe degree has in common

YEAR 1

First-Year Seminar

A single small seminar in argument and evidence, capped at 15, chosen from a rotating list of topics.

YEARS 1–2

Distribution Requirement

Two courses in each of the four divisions outside a student's declared major.

YEAR 4

Senior Thesis

An independent project defended before a three-person faculty committee each spring.
